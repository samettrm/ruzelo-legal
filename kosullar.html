Last updated: 24-11-2026 · BUSATER YAZILIM A.Ş. · bsdigitalapp+ruzelo@gmail.com

1. What Ruzelo can and cannot do

Ruzelo looks for signs of hidden cameras and trackers: devices on the Wi-Fi network that behave like cameras, Bluetooth trackers, lens reflections, infrared light and magnetic changes. Each result shows the reason behind it. A scan cannot rule out cameras that are switched off, not connected to the network you are on, recording to a memory card, or hidden from view. A clean report is not a guarantee that a room is free of cameras.

2. Lawful use

Use Ruzelo only on networks and in places you are allowed to check. Do not use it to access, disrupt or interfere with other people's devices. If you find something, do not tamper with it; contact the property owner, the platform you booked through, or the police.

3. Free features

Two full room scans per day are free. Verdicts and their reasons are always visible in free scans.

4. Ruzelo Pro

  • Ruzelo Pro is optional and sold through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone and iPad) as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. The price is shown by the store before purchase, and payment is charged to your Google Play or Apple account.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before renewal. You can cancel at any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or on iPhone and iPad in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • There is no free trial.
  • Refunds are handled under the refund policy of the store you bought from (Google Play or Apple). When a purchase is refunded or revoked, Pro access ends.
  • You can restore purchases on a new device with "Restore".
